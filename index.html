About Me

I am a PhD student at Colorado State University. My research focuses on usable security, privacy, and human-centered cybersecurity.

My current research examines how people make privacy decisions when interacting with smart home technologies, with a particular interest in the perspectives of both smart home owners and guests.

Research

Smart Home Privacy

Studying how smart home owners make privacy-setting decisions and balance their own privacy preferences with the preferences of guests.

Perspective-Taking & Privacy

Exploring whether imagining oneself as a guest in another smart home influences privacy-setting decisions.

Usable Security

Investigating human-centered approaches to security and privacy in technology-mediated environments.
